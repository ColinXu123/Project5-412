Weeknight shoyu ramen

Not a 12-hour broth, but a satisfying bowl in about 30 minutes. Serves 2.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cloves garlic and a thumb of ginger, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 portions fresh ramen noodles
  • 2 eggs
  • Scallions, nori, and any leftover roast pork or chicken

Steps

  1. Jammy eggs: Boil the eggs for 6.5 minutes, then move them to ice water. Peel and halve.
  2. Broth: Simmer the stock with garlic and ginger for 15 minutes, then strain.
  3. Tare: Split the soy sauce, mirin, and sesame oil between two bowls.
  4. Noodles: Cook the noodles according to the package, usually 1 to 2 minutes, and drain well.
  5. Assemble: Pour hot broth over the tare, stir, add noodles, and top with egg, meat, scallions, and nori.

Eat right away, before the noodles soften. Slurping is encouraged.
